GameIdea Competitive Scan

A game concept generator. The user sets filters, then draws one card from each concept deck. The user writes a one or two sentence hook from the cards.

App
Card-draw tool for game ideas. Filters are Genre, Format and Style. Core decks are Player Role, Mechanic, Goal, Conflict and Resource. Optional decks are Setting, Mood, Twist, Progression and Constraint. A Modifier card stacks an adjective on a Role, Resource or Setting card. The output is a hook. A hook is one or two sentences that make a person want to play the game. Source: concept.md.
Related app by the same user
TheForge, a story-card canvas for writers. concept.md names its Description card as the model for the Modifier card. TheForge is the user's own app. This scan does not treat it as a comparable.

How to read this

Only the Required bucket feeds MVP scope directly. Required rows are table stakes. The app is not what it claims without them.

Top 10 and Other are the reviewed backlog. dev-add-feature takes rows from them one at a time, against a working app. This document does not decide scope. dev-initial-interview decides scope and marks each row.

Sentiment items name their source. "None found" means the research found no user comments. Independent reviews in this category are few. Most pages about AI tools are written by vendors. Reddit pages were blocked to the research tools. Items marked UNVERIFIED came from a search summary only. The best user evidence is in itch.io comment threads.

Market-wide signals

  1. Random tools give nonsense. AI tools give generic ideas. Users of word-list generators post contradictory results. Examples are "relaxing and permadeath" and "kill all the enemies but you cant fight" (SnowyOwl generator comments). One user praises a generator because it is "not at all nonsensical (most other generators often generate nonsense)" (monochroma380 page, UNVERIFIED). The AI side gets the opposite complaint. A developer called Ludo's ideas "derivative." The reviewer writes that "Generated concepts can feel familiar" (Soonlab review). Some users like the contradictions. The SnowyOwl author says "the most interesting ideas were the ones that seem contradictory at first." A tool that lets the user control how strange a draw is answers both complaints.
  2. The result is a dead end. Most free generators give a list of words and stop. Vendors now sell the next step. Summer Engine says "a lone random word leaves you exactly where you began" (Summer Engine, vendor claim). Wayline sends an idea to a design document tool. Ludo moves an idea into a project workspace. One user did not use the generated idea, but "it just made my brain suddenly think of something" (SnowyOwl comments). A tool that turns the draw into a hook gives the user a result they can use.
  3. Saving needs an account or money. Wayline Ignite saves only for signed-in users (Wayline). The Story Shack saves favorites only with a free account (Story Shack). Ludo runs on credits after 30 free ones (Soonlab). The itch.io generators save nothing. A tool that saves draws locally with no account answers this.
  4. The user cannot aim the draw. The Story Shack FAQ says the user cannot focus on a genre yet and must keep rolling (Story Shack). A beginner says the ideas get "a tad too big for me just starting out small" (SnowyOwl comments). Category lists also mix up terms. A user asked "Pixel art is a game genre?" and the author agreed to fix the list (monochroma380 comments). The Genre, Format and Style filters in concept.md answer this. The genre tag problem in concept.md is the same problem.

Required Table stakes · unranked

One draw fills a fixed set of concept slots In MVP · P8
Every comparable composes an idea from slots. Wayline: Genre, Setting, Primary Mechanic, Objective, Unusual Mechanic. Summer Engine: genre, setting, core system, twist. Jam Mixer: theme, mechanic, twist, constraint. What the Deck and Gamestorm: Genre, Mechanics/Hazards, World/Environment, Story/Setting, Characters/Enemies.
Sentiment: Positive on the itch.io tools. "Really useful tool especially for game jams" (monochroma380 comments). The SnowyOwl generator has a 4.8 rating from 34 ratings (itch.io).
In MVP, milestone 8. Each deck is a slot, and the Hook Card joins the slots into one idea. From milestone 2 the user draws one card at a time. From milestone 8, Deal Stack on the Hook Card fills every missing slot in 1 click: the 5 base decks in core order, and on a One-Pager board 2 Details chips each of 7 decks. Redraw All refills a stack that already exists. See feature-deal-a-stack.html.
Redraw all slots in one action In MVP · P2
Wayline "Shuffle a new idea," monochroma380 "Randomize All," Summer Engine reroll, Story Shack new result on each click.
Sentiment: Users ask for it when it is missing. "you should add a flip all button that resets all the cards. it gets annoying clicking all of them to restart" (SnowyOwl comments).
In MVP, milestone 2. Redraw All on the Hook Card replaces every unlocked card in its stack.
Redraw one slot In MVP · P2
Wayline (tap a field), monochroma380 "Randomize One," Jam Mixer single panel, SnowyOwl (flip a card back). Physical decks tell the user to swap out cards that do not inspire them.
Sentiment: None found as a separate comment.
In MVP, milestone 2. Redraw on the card strip replaces one card in place and keeps its tucks and lock state.
Lock a slot so a redraw keeps it In MVP · P2
Wayline, monochroma380, Jam Mixer, Summer Engine ("keep the parts you like"), oddpotatodev. A lock keeps a card in place while the rest change.
Sentiment: None found as a separate comment. Lock is near-universal across the digital tools.
In MVP, milestone 2. Each card has a lock. Redraw All skips locked cards.
Show the draw as one readable sentence In MVP · P2
SnowyOwl shows "a readable version of the game idea" when all cards are face up. Summer Engine gives a hook and a core loop. Story Shack joins parts into a theme.
Sentiment: Users quote the joined sentence to share results, for example "a cute game, but its a MURDER MYSTERY" (SnowyOwl comments).
In MVP, milestone 2. The Hook Card shows the stack as a sentence, joined by connector badges.
Copy the result out In MVP · P2
Jam Mixer copies the prompt with one click, or the C key on desktop. Story Shack copies a theme on click.
Sentiment: None found.
In MVP, milestone 2. Copy on the Hook Card puts the sentence on the clipboard as plain text.
Free to use with no account In MVP · P1
All itch.io generators are free with no sign-in. Wayline and Story Shack let the user draw with no account. The Deck of Lenses app is free with no in-app purchases.
Sentiment: Physical decks cost money. What the Deck is $63.99 (The Game Crafter). See market-wide signal 3.
In MVP, milestone 1. The app runs locally with no account, no server and no cost.
Core deck set: genre, mechanic, setting, goal and a twist or constraint In MVP · P4
Every comparable covers most of these five. Mechanic and setting are in every one. Goal is in Wayline, oddpotatodev and SnowyOwl. Twist is in Summer Engine, Jam Mixer, Story Shack and oddpotatodev.
Sentiment: Users suggest more entries. "You can add Murder Mystery, Couch Co-op and Co-op" (monochroma380 comments).
In MVP. Mechanic and Goal ship in milestone 2. Setting, Twist and Constraint ship in milestone 4. Genre is a setting on each deck, from milestone 3.

Top 10 Differentiators · ranked

  1. Coherence control: avoid nonsense draws, or ask for strange ones Not in MVP
    oddpotatodev has a "basic compatibility check." Matthew Norman's generator aims for ideas that "make a little bit of sense." Summer Engine lets the user push results "weirder or more grounded." Most word-list tools have no control.
    Sentiment: The loudest complaint in the category. "'relaxing and permadeath' are not two things that belong together" and "kill all the enemies, but you cant fight (so i just wait this out?)" (SnowyOwl comments). Some users like the clashes. "wdym thats the perfect combo" (same thread).
    Rank 1. Complaint signal is the strongest found. It applies to every random comparable. It fits the deck design in concept.md, because cards from five decks can clash.
    Not in MVP. The plan answers part of the need with curated pools, genre settings, rotation of 2 or 4 cues and the lock. The M2 hook test measures how often stacks read as nonsense. No control for "weirder or more grounded" is planned.
  2. Save and revisit draws with no account In MVP · P1
    Wayline saves to a workspace for signed-in users. Story Shack saves favorites to "Chest" with an account. The itch.io tools save nothing.
    Sentiment: No direct user comment found. The gating is documented on the vendor pages (Wayline, Story Shack).
    Rank 2. Market-wide signal 3. Every tool that saves puts it behind an account. A hook is worth less if the user cannot find it later.
    In MVP, milestone 1. Autosave and multiple boards carry over from TheForge. Data stays in the browser with no account.
  3. Genre filter that limits the draw In MVP · P3
    Summer Engine lets the user ask for a genre or avoid one. Ludo starts from a theme, genre or platform. Story Shack has no genre focus. Wayline draws genre as a slot. It is not a filter.
    Sentiment: Story Shack FAQ admits the gap: the user must keep rolling until a theme fits (Story Shack).
    Rank 3. A documented gap in a comparable, and the only steering tools are AI tools. It matches the open genre tag problem in concept.md.
    In MVP, milestone 3. 21 genres, plus a hidden Generic tag, on the 4 genre-tagged decks: Mechanic, Resource, Progression and Constraint. The Genres panel sets them together and one by one, at any time. A change affects the next draw only. The other 7 decks ignore genre.
  4. Clean, curated card pools with consistent categories In MVP · P3
    monochroma380 hand-wrote word lists per category. SnowyOwl has about 80 to 120 entries by a user's guess. Physical decks are curated by design: What the Deck has 259 cards and Gamestorm more than 300.
    Sentiment: "Pixel art is a game genre?" The author answered "i did list aesthetic as genre. I'll redo the word list" (monochroma380 comments). "cute is a genre in this? lol" (SnowyOwl comments).
    Rank 4. Repeated complaints on two popular tools. The Genre and Style split in concept.md answers the exact error users reported.
    In MVP, milestones 2 to 4. The user writes every library in docs/libraries/. A one-time conversion makes the JSON pools. A cue rule keeps genre and art style apart.
  5. Turn the draw into a pitch: hook, stakes and angle Partial · P2
    Summer Engine gives "a concept with a hook and a core loop." Wayline expands an idea for signed-in users. Ludo develops mechanics, characters and story in a project. The free word-list tools stop at the words.
    Sentiment: Vendor claims only. Summer Engine says other generators give "a string of disconnected words" (Summer Engine). A user says the ideas help even when unused (SnowyOwl comments).
    Rank 5. Market-wide signal 2. Three paid or AI tools sell this step, and no free card tool has it. The hook template in concept.md is this row.
    Partial, milestone 2. The Hook Card sentence gives who, what you do and stakes. The angle comes only from Twist or Constraint cards. An editable angle line, a core loop and a concept sheet are left out.
  6. Scope filter: project size, session length, player count Never
    Steven the Gamer adds "a limitation." Jam Mixer adds a constraint panel. Ludo starts from a platform. No tool found filters by project size.
    Sentiment: "sometimes the game gets a tad too big for me just starting out small" (SnowyOwl comments).
    Rank 6. One clear user complaint, and no comparable fills it. It fits the Format filter in concept.md.
    Never. The plan rules out Format in any form. The user's reason: indie developers already know their format, so it does not narrow a draw. The Constraint deck gives design limits such as "one room".
  7. State that the user owns the generated ideas Not in MVP
    SnowyOwl answers each request in comments. No comparable states it on the page.
    Sentiment: Three users asked permission. "permission to use this in my game?", "Can I use the idea? I'll credit you," and "would you mind if I published something made from this?" The author replied "any ideas you get from this are completely yours" (SnowyOwl comments).
    Rank 7. Users ask for it directly and repeatedly. It costs one line of text.
    Not in MVP. Not discussed in the interview. It costs one line of text and is a cheap later add.
  8. Show comparable shipped games for a draw Not in MVP
    Wayline shows "similar shipped games" to signed-in users. Ludo sells market research. concept.md names an optional comparison part, such as "Stardew Valley meets Dark Souls."
    Sentiment: None found.
    Rank 8. Two paid or account tools offer it, so it is a feature the market values. No user sentiment was found.
    Not in MVP. Deferred in the plan.
  9. Question prompts that test an idea after the draw Not in MVP
    The Art of Game Design: a Deck of Lenses has 113 lens cards. Each card asks key questions about a design. The Serious Games Idea Deck and the Mixed Reality Game Cards use question cards too.
    Sentiment: 4.6 from 18 ratings. "a great tool to hone, adjust, or modify any video or board game design" (App Store). The same page calls the app design "archaic" and asks for "a search function." The app was last updated in 2016.
    Rank 9. Strong praise from professionals, but for a different step. It tests an idea after the draw. The only app is old and gets interface complaints.
    Not in MVP.
  10. Send a saved idea to a design document Partial · P7
    Wayline sends ideas to Blueprint to make a game design document. Ludo builds one in its project workspace. Milanote has a game design document template board.
    Sentiment: None found from users. Milanote's guide says long text design documents often "end up unused" (Milanote, vendor claim).
    Rank 10. Common in the paid tools, with no user sentiment found. It extends market-wide signal 2.
    Partial, milestone 7. The One-Pager board holds 1 game: its hook and a Concept panel of the other cards by deck. The text file lists both. Sending to an outside design document tool, and a One-Pager export, are still left out. See feature-one-pager-board.html.

Other Backlog · unranked

Works in a mobile browser Not in MVP
monochroma380 runs on mobile. SnowyOwl is HTML5 and has layout problems.
Sentiment: "runs in a mobile browser, 5 stars" (monochroma380 comments). "it zoomed in to a corner near the top, and I couldn't see or click on the lower half of the cards" (SnowyOwl comments).
Not in MVP. The plan is desktop browsers only. Tablet and touch input are deferred.
AI concept art or mood images for a draw Not in MVP
Ludo generates images, 3D, audio and video. Wayline offers concept art and sound direction to signed-in users.
Sentiment: Ludo assets can "feel as if they came from different games" (Soonlab).
Not in MVP. The MVP has no AI.
Generate a playable prototype from the idea Not in MVP
Summer Engine builds a Godot project. Ludo makes playable iterations at 5 credits each. Rosebud AI also builds games from prompts.
Sentiment: A Reddit user reported a simple game "stayed buggy," and each fix cost five credits (Soonlab, reporting a Reddit thread).
Not in MVP.

Group brainstorming with cards Not in MVP
Design Space Cards and the Mixed Reality Game Cards are research decks for groups. They use rules for draws, turn-taking and discards. The Serious Games Idea Deck is collaborative.
Sentiment: In a controlled study, Design Space Cards did not speed up ideation, but participants preferred them (TU Delft, search summary, UNVERIFIED).
Not in MVP. One user, no server.

Partial, milestone 1. The infinite canvas, cards and titled notes carry over from TheForge. Milestone 7 adds the One-Pager board, a structured board kind with a Hook Train and a Concept panel (feature-one-pager-board.html). Images and moodboards are still left out.

Not in MVP. The pools ship with the app and the user cannot change them.

Modifier card that stacks an adjective on a card In MVP · P2
Added from the interview. No comparable found. concept.md defines it. The model is the Description card combine in TheForge, the user's own app.
