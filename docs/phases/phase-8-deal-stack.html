Phase 8: Deal A Stack

The following plan should be complete, but validate documentation, codebase patterns, and task sanity before implementing. Pay special attention to naming of existing utils, types, and models — import from the right files.

Phase Description

Phase 8 adds Deal Stack to the Hook Card hover menu. One click fills every missing deal slot of that Hook Card:

  • Base deal (both board kinds): 1 card each of Player Role, Mechanic, Resource, Goal and Conflict in the Hook Train, each in its core-order position.
  • Details deal (One-Pager boards only): 2 chips each in the Player Role, Resource, Setting, Mood, Twist, Progression and Constraint lanes of the Details panel (14 chips; 19 cards in a full One-Pager deal).
  • Fill rule: only missing slots are dealt. Nothing the user placed moves out of the train or is replaced.
  • No repeated cues: a dealt card avoids every cue already showing on a card of the same deck on the board, and the cues dealt earlier in the same deal, with a fallback when the pool is too short.
  • Reveal: the deal is 1 board change (1 undo step, 1 save); the dealt cards then fade in one at a time over about 1 second, at once with reduced motion.
  • Icons: Deal Stack takes playing-cards-fan; Redraw (card strip) and Redraw All (Hook Card menu) change to a newly vendored square-arrow-down.

There is no data model change: dealt cards are ordinary cards, the board format stays at version 2, and no Dexie version or upgrade step is added. Behavior authority: docs/feature-deal-a-stack.html. Visual authority: docs/design-system.html 7, 9.4, 9.5, 9.9 and 11.

User Stories

  • As a game jam entrant, I want to deal a full core stack under a Hook Card in one click, so that I see a complete hook sentence in seconds and spend my time turning, locking and redrawing instead of clicking and dragging 11 times.
  • As a solo developer on a One-Pager board, I want the same click to fill the Details panel with 2 cards in each detail lane, so that the one-pager has a starting point in every lane.
  • As a user with a half-built stack, I want a deal to add only the missing cards in their reading positions, so that nothing I placed is lost or reordered.
  • As a user, I want Redraw and Redraw All to look different from Deal Stack, so that I can tell "replace" from "fill" at a glance.

Problem Statement

A full core stack today takes 6 rail clicks and 5 drags, and a One-Pager board needs 14 more draws and drops into the panel. The PRD's first user story asks for a full concept stack in about a minute, and the Required scan row "One draw fills a fixed set of concept slots" is only Partial.

Solution Statement

A pure dealStack(board, hookId, draw) in features/canvas/boardActions.ts computes the missing slots, draws each card through a caller-supplied draw(deck, excluded), inserts base cards into the train by the core-order insert rule (a small insertUnder helper in stack.ts), adds Details cards with enterConcept, and returns the new board plus the dealt ids in reveal order. canDeal drives the menu item's disabled state. The draw change is an optional excluded set in DrawOptions. The canvas runs the deal as 1 changeBoard and keeps a view-only reveal map (id → delay ms) that the card nodes and Details chips read for a CSS fade with a per-card animation-delay; reduced motion turns the animation off.

Patterns to Follow

Multi-card recipe with a caller-supplied draw (boardActions.ts 236–250):

export function redrawAll(
  board: Board,
  hookId: string,
  draw: (card: Card) => Card,
): { board: Board; redrawn: string[] } {
  let next = board;
  const redrawn: string[] = [];
  for (const card of descendantsOf(board, hookId)) {
    if (card.locked || card.type === 'hook' || card.inConcept) continue;
    next = redrawCard(next, card.id, draw(card));
    redrawn.push(card.id);
  }
  return { board: next, redrawn };
}

redrawAll: (hookId: string) => {

Edge Cases

  • A train with cards out of core order (Goal above Role): every missing card is inserted after the last earlier-core card in reading order; no card leaves the train.
  • A Hook Card whose first child is a non-core card (Hook → Twist): a dealt Player Role tucks right under the Hook Card and the Twist moves under it.
  • A combined Player Role in the train counts as present; its adjective ring is not part of the Player Role exclusion (it came from a Modifier deck).
  • A Modifier tucked under the Hook Card stays its child and still reads first.
  • A lane with 3 or more chips gets none; a Details lane for a deck outside the 7 (Mechanic, Goal, Conflict, Modifier) is never dealt.
  • Pools still loading, or failed: Deal Stack disabled; the action also returns early.
  • Genre filter narrowed to 1 genre: dealt Mechanic, Resource, Progression and Constraint cues all carry it.
  • A deal while a previous reveal is still running: the new reveal map replaces the old one and its timer restarts.
  • A deal on a Hook Card near other loose cards: the train lays out by the tuck rule and may overlap loose cards, as a normal tuck does.

Acceptance Criteria
