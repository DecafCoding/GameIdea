The Hook Card

The Hook Card is the head of a stack. It is dark, so it stands apart from the cream cue cards. It reads the stack from top to bottom and shows the sentence. The sentence updates after each change and is never stored.

A. New Hook Card
Player Role goes here
Hook

Tuck a Player Role under this card. Then tuck a Mechanic, a Resource, a Goal and a Conflict.

The user draws it from the top of the rail. Its menu holds Copy and Redraw All. They stay off until a card is tucked under it.
B. Selected, after Copy
Hook

You are a lonely ghost pilot who learns to fly to reach the last city, but fuel runs out at sunset. It feels hopeful. Except the city is empty.

The menu shows only when the user clicks the Hook Card. Copy puts the sentence on the clipboard as plain text and shows Copied for 2 seconds. Mood, Twist and Progression start a new sentence.
