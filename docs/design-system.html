3. Base system and deliberate deviations

The base is the TheForge visual design. It gives GameIdea:

  • A dark canvas with a 24 px dot grid.
  • Space Grotesk for display text and Inter for body text.
  • A top bar 56 px tall and a left rail 232 px wide.
  • Cream square cards, 200 px, radius 14.
  • A ring control around the center icon. It shows on hover, and a click on a ring half turns it one step.
  • Tuck: a lower card shows a 56 px strip below the card above it.
  • Free-text notes on cream paper.
  • A card strip menu above a card. In GameIdea it shows on hover, 4 px above the card user 2026-11-03.
  • Buttons 44 px tall.

Deliberate deviations. This list is closed: keep all of them, and add none.

  1. Rail buttons have no icon circle. The icon is drawn in its deck rail color, and buttons are 36 px tall, not 44 px. Reason: the rail holds every deck button and must fit on one screen, and mouse-only desktop use makes 36 px acceptable.
  2. Deck colors are darkened for white text. Every card color passes 4.5:1 with white badge text, and each deck has a lighter rail color for dark surfaces. Reason: accessibility.
  3. GameIdea decks replace the TheForge story decks. There are 11 decks; the Modifier deck draws as 3 rail buttons (Role, Resource, Setting) that share one purple and differ by icon.
  4. The Hook Card heads each stack: 240 × 200 with a 2 px theme color border. It is dark (#2A2F38) in dark mode and white (#FFFFFF) in light mode 2026-11-04. Reason: cards follow the mode now, so the border carries the Hook Card's identity in both modes.
  5. Hook Card actions sit in a strip menu above the card, not on the card. Reason: less clutter, and it matches the other cards. It opens on hover user 2026-11-02.
  6. Connector badges are pills in the lower card's color. This was TheForge's option C.
  7. Genre counts replace a genre mark. A 15 px pill in the theme color on the 4 genre decks shows how many genres are on.
  8. Themes. There are Dark and Light modes, and Orange, Blue and Green theme colors; Orange dark is the default. The theme color changes only the accent. The mode changes the chrome, card faces, tucked faces, the note and the Hook Card fill 2026-11-04. Deck colors, rings, badges and center icons never change. This reverses TheForge's "one theme, one accent" rule. Reason: cream cards on a dark canvas were too bright, and the user asked for dark cards.
  9. A Settings button with a gear icon sits at the bottom of the rail.
  10. Concept chips are text-sized pills with radius 8, not cards 2026-11-04. Reason: many settled cards must fit beside the Hook Card on a One-Pager board.
  11. Genres live in a Settings tab, not in their own panel and rail button 2026-11-04. Reason: fewer rail items, and genres are a setting the user changes rarely.

The list was closed at 9 items on 2026-11-02. The 2026-11-04 handoff changed 4 and 8 and added 10 and 11, with the user's agreement. It is closed again at 11.

4. Color

The theme contract sets 2 attributes on <html> before the first render: the mode (dark | light) and the theme color (orange | blue | green). The attributes are data-mode and data-theme user 2026-11-02. All chrome colors come from tokens that these 2 attributes select. The card face, Hook Card and note tokens (4.5) follow data-mode only 2026-11-04. Deck, ring, badge and center icon colors (4.4) depend on neither attribute.

10. Cross-cutting interaction contracts

  1. Rings show on hover only. The card body and center icon never rotate. A click on a ring half turns that ring one step; R turns the outer or only ring, and Shift+R turns the inner ring.
  2. Every card shows its strip menu on hover, plain cards and the Hook Card alike user 2026-11-03. A click selects a plain card (outline, Delete, Esc).
  3. Every user action is one undo step: draw, rotate, tuck, combine, swap, On Top, split, redraw, Redraw All, lock and unlock, discard, a badge word change, a drop with or without snap, Add note, and on a One-Pager board a drop into Concept, a pop-out, a drag out and a Concept resize. Settings, Settings tab changes and genre changes are not in the undo history.
  4. Nothing changes cards already on the board except a direct action on them. A genre change affects the next draw only.
  5. The Hook sentence is computed, never stored, and never shows a blank slot.
  6. Destructive actions: Discard is undoable and has no dialog. Delete board is not undoable and always asks.
  7. Snap to grid applies only to the drop of a free card or note. Alt held at the drop places it freely. Tuck, combine and On Top drops ignore snap.
  8. Each notice names what happened and offers one action.
  9. Theme and mode 2026-11-04: the theme color changes only the accent. The mode changes the chrome, card faces, tucked faces, the note and the Hook Card fill. Neither changes a badge, ring, center icon or deck color.
