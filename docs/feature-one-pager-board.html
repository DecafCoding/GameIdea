Feature — One-Pager Board

2026-11-04 · Extends GameIdea (working name), as planned in mvp-plan.html. Written from the /dev-add-feature run. Lands in Phase 7.

Visual authority: design-system.html, 9.15 holds the look of the Concept panel, lanes, chips, resize handle and chip drop target, added from the 2026-11-04 handoff. The board kind choice in New Board, the One-Pager empty state, the rail Hook Card button on a One-Pager board and the board list label are not drawn yet (design-system section 15). Where this brief and the design doc disagree on a visual detail, the design doc wins.

Summary and motivation

A game idea often has more than one Resource, Setting, Mechanic or Goal. Today every card goes into one vertical train under a Hook Card. At about 10 cards the train is too tall to see or edit. The hook sentence also becomes a paragraph instead of a hook.

This feature adds a second board kind, the One-Pager board. It holds exactly 1 game. It has 1 Hook Card with a short Hook Train, and 1 Concept panel for every other card. The Concept panel groups its cards by deck as small chips, so many cards take little space.

The current board becomes the Free Form board. It does not change.

Fit with the existing application

The feature branches the core loop. On a One-Pager board, the user still draws, turns rings, combines and tucks under the Hook Card. The user also commits settled cards to the Concept panel. The hook sentence reads only the Hook Train, as today.

The name "One-Pager" comes from the game industry. A one-pager is a single page that opens with the high concept and then lists what the game has. Here the board itself is that page, because the board holds 1 game only.

The feature partly answers 2 rows of the competitive scan: "Send a saved idea to a design document" and "Visual board to develop an idea".

Related docs: mvp-plan.html, prd.html, architecture.html, competitive-scan.html, feature-modifier-decks.html, feature-settings-and-theme.html.

Scope

In this feature

  • 2 board kinds: Free Form (the current board, unchanged) and One-Pager. The user picks the kind in the New Board dialog. The names are "One-Pager" and "Free Form" in every place.
  • A One-Pager board starts with its 1 Hook Card already placed. It can never have a second Hook Card.
  • The Hook Card on a One-Pager board has Copy Hook and Redraw All only. It has no Duplicate and no Discard.
  • The Hook Train: the stack under the Hook Card, with the same tuck rules and hook sentence as today.
  • The Concept panel: 1 for each One-Pager board, at a fixed place to the right of the Hook Card. It moves when the Hook Card moves. It has no link line, because the board has only 1 Hook Card and 1 Concept panel.
  • The panel width is resizable with a handle on its right edge, from 232 to 640 with no snap. The height grows with its content. The width is saved with the board.
  • Lanes: 1 lane for each deck that has a card in the panel, in rail order. A lane shows only while it has a card. During a drag, the lane for the dragged card's deck shows.
  • Chips: each Concept card shows as a chip with its active text and no icon. Chips wrap side by side in their lane, in drop order. A chip is never wider than its lane, and long text wraps onto more lines. Each lane has a label with the deck icon and the plural deck name.
  • A card dropped anywhere on the panel goes into the lane of its deck. Any deck can go into the panel. A Hook Card and a note cannot.
  • Chips are settled. A chip has no buttons: no rotate, lock, redraw or discard.
  • A double-click on a chip moves its card out of the panel to an open place on the canvas beside the panel. A drag of a chip onto the canvas also moves it out. Out of the panel, the card is a full card again.
  • A Modifier dropped on a Player Role, Resource or Setting chip combines with it, by the rules of feature-modifier-decks.html. The adjective that faces down at drop time is the one used. The drop hint names the result, for example "Stolen Salt". A Modifier dropped on the panel but not on such a chip stays on the canvas.
  • Redraw All changes only the Hook Train. It never changes Concept cards.
  • Loose cards and notes sit on the open canvas, as on a Free Form board.
  • The text file of a One-Pager board has a Concept section: a bullet list for each deck, in lane order.
  • The board list shows a One-Pager label on One-Pager boards.
  • The footer on a One-Pager board reads, for example, "12 cards". The card count is the Hook Train plus the Concept cards. It does not count the Hook Card or loose cards.
  • Board format version 2, with an upgrade step. Every existing board becomes a Free Form board.

Deferred

  • A One-Pager export or a Copy One-Pager item. The text file covers the need for now.
  • Make One-Pager: a Hook Card item on a Free Form board that makes a new One-Pager board from that stack.
  • More than 1 train on a One-Pager board, for example for levels.
  • Order changes inside a lane. To change the order, the user moves chips out and drops them back in.
  • Keyboard moves into and out of the Concept panel. Tuck is mouse-only today.

Never

  • A change of kind on an existing board, in either direction.
  • A second Hook Card or a second Concept panel on a One-Pager board.
  • A Playground box. The open canvas does that job.
  • Side tucks and sentence columns. The brainstorm ruled them out: side tucks looked too busy, and sentence columns do not make the train shorter.

Data model changes

Board
  formatVersion: 2
  kind: 'free' | 'onePager'          required
  conceptWidth?: number              One-Pager boards only. The Concept panel width in px.
  ...every version 1 field

Card
  inConcept: boolean                 required. True while the card is in the Concept panel.
  ...every version 1 field

Rules

  • A One-Pager board has exactly 1 Hook Card.
  • A card with inConcept: true has tuckedUnder: null. Its x and y stay stored and are not used while it is in the panel.
  • A Hook Card never has inConcept: true.
  • On a Free Form board, every card has inConcept: false, and conceptWidth is absent.
  • Chip order in a lane is z, from low to high. A card gets a new top z when it goes into the panel, as a tuck does today. No new order field is needed.
  • An On Top Modifier keeps its onTopOf on a Concept card. It has no place of its own, as today.
  • When a card moves out of the panel, inConcept becomes false and the card gets a new x and y on the canvas.

Migration and rollback

  • IndexedDB: Dexie schema version 2. Its upgrade sets formatVersion: 2 and kind: 'free' on each board record, and inConcept: false on each card. The boardIndex table does not change. The board list already loads each full board for its summary.
  • Board files: UPGRADES[1] in features/export/upgrade.ts does the same change to a version 1 file on import. It does not set conceptWidth, because every version 1 board is Free Form.
  • Rollback: after the Dexie upgrade runs, an older build cannot open the database. The user exports the real boards on port 4174 before the first run of the Phase 7 build. A version 2 board file does not import into an older build.

Interfaces and contracts

New Board dialog

The dialog asks for the name and the kind: One-Pager or Free Form. The layout and the default kind come from /dev-ui-update.

Drop routing on a One-Pager board

DraggedDropped onResult
Any deck card except a ModifierAnywhere on the Concept panelGoes into the lane of its deck. Its lane shows during the drag.
A ModifierA Player Role, Resource or Setting chipCombines with that chip. The hint names the result.
A ModifierAnother place on the panelStays on the canvas where it was released.
A Hook Card or a noteThe Concept panelNot allowed. No drop hint shows.
A chipThe canvasLeaves the panel and becomes a full card at the drop point.
A chipA card in the Hook TrainLeaves the panel and tucks there, by the normal tuck rules.

A card that has cards tucked under it brings them with it into the panel. Each of them becomes a chip in its own lane.

Chip actions

  • Double-click: the card leaves the panel and goes to an open place beside the panel, from findOpenSpot.
  • Drag: as in the drop table.
  • No other action. The ring of a chip cannot turn.

Undo and save

  • Each of these is 1 undo step and 1 save: a drop into the panel, a pop-out, a drag out, a combine onto a chip, and a panel resize. A resize saves once, on release, as a note resize does.
  • Redraw All stays 1 undo step. It changes no Concept card.

Text file

Hook
You are a reanimated lighthouse keeper who must trade using stolen memories to keep the light on, but the tide rises every night.

Concept
Mechanics
- repair
Resources
- stolen salt
- favors

The Concept section comes after the hook and before Notes. The lanes come in rail order and the chips in lane order. Each bullet is the card's words on the board, with its merged and On Top adjectives. The exact headings come from the existing text file format.

Board list and footer

  • A One-Pager board shows a One-Pager label and its hook sentence.
  • The footer reads "N cards". N is the Hook Train plus the Concept cards. It does not count the Hook Card or loose cards.

Compatibility and rollout

  • Every existing board opens as a Free Form board and behaves as before.
  • There is no feature flag. The app has 1 user and runs locally.
  • Before the first Phase 7 build runs on port 4174, the user exports each real board as a board file.
  • Phase 6 (Themed Cards) comes first, so the chips and the panel are built once against the final card tokens in both modes.

Testing and regression plan

Unit tests (Vitest)

  • The Dexie version 2 upgrade and UPGRADES[1]: version 1 records and files become Free Form boards with every card inConcept: false.
  • Zod: a good One-Pager file passes. Each bad case fails: 0 Hook Cards, 2 Hook Cards, a Concept card with tuckedUnder, and a Hook Card in Concept.
  • Drop routing: each deck goes to its own lane. A Modifier combines only on a Player Role, Resource or Setting chip.
  • Lane order follows rail order. Chip order follows z.
  • Pop-out places the card at an open spot. Drag out and pop-out set inConcept to false.
  • Redraw All leaves every Concept card unchanged.
  • Stack order and hook sentences skip Concept cards.
  • The text file Concept section, and the footer counts.
  • A new One-Pager board has exactly 1 Hook Card.

Browser tests (Playwright, 3 engines)

  • Make a One-Pager board from New Board. It opens with its Hook Card.
  • Drop a card into the panel. Pop it out by double-click. Drag it back in and drag it out. Undo restores each step.
  • Combine a Modifier onto a chip. The chip reads with the adjective.
  • Resize the panel and reload. The width stays.
  • Move the Hook Card. The panel moves with it.
  • Import a version 1 board file. It opens as a Free Form board.

Regression

  • Every existing unit and browser test passes on Free Form boards.
  • The 200-card and 11-card stack speed checks still pass.

Done criteria

  • Every existing board opens unchanged as a Free Form board after the upgrade. Every existing test passes.
  • New Board makes a One-Pager board or a Free Form board.
  • On a One-Pager board, the user can drop each deck into the panel, combine a Modifier onto a chip, pop a card out by double-click and by drag, and resize the panel. Each action is 1 undo step.
  • Redraw All leaves the Concept panel unchanged.
  • The text file shows the Concept section.
  • A bad One-Pager board file fails on import with the normal message, and nothing changes.
  • The speed, size and privacy targets in architecture.html still pass.

Open risks and assumptions

Risks

  • The first format upgrade. It is the first real use of the upgrade path. A mistake can stop saved boards from loading. The tests come first, and the user exports real boards before the first run.
  • Settled chips can feel rigid. A chip cannot turn. To change one, the user moves it out. If this feels slow in use, a later change can add a pop-over editor.
  • Drop targets in a small panel. A Modifier must hit a chip, and chips are small. The drop hint must show clearly which chip is the target.

Assumptions

  • A chip can be dragged out as well as popped out by double-click.
  • A card with cards tucked under it brings them into the panel, each in its own lane.
  • Phase 6 (Themed Cards) lands before Phase 7.
